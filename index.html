About

Shaping ideas
through lens & code

I'm Harish Kumar S — a 2nd-year B.Tech student in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science at Rajalakshmi Engineering College, splitting my time between coursework and hands-on creative work.

Outside the classroom, I serve as the official video editor for both my Leo Club and ARQ Club, where I combine technical editing skill with storytelling for community service and club initiatives — cutting, grading, and pacing footage that holds people's attention.

My interests sit at the intersection of visual craft and technical building. I edit video and photos with an eye for pacing and color, and I build websites and write code in Python, C, and Java. I like projects where those two sides meet — because a well-shot piece still needs a good edit, and a good site still needs a good eye.
